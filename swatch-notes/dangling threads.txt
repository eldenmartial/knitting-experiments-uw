The tube: Knit circularly by alternating passes on the front and back beds.
The loops: Knit the loop yarn (on a separate carrier) on extra needles beside or between the tube needles, then drop those stitches at the end.
The yarn ladders out into loops.
To make loops longer, space the loop needles farther apart or drop wider groups.
To keep the loops from pulling out, tuck the loop yarn into the tube needles every so often.
